<task>
Evaluate the following client application against six mandatory risk dimensions and produce a structured risk assessment.

The six dimensions are:
1. entity_structure_complexity — How complex is the legal/ownership structure? (layers of holding companies, trusts, multiple entities, nominee directors, etc.)
2. jurisdiction_risk — What is the FATF/regulatory risk of the client's jurisdiction(s)? (offshore registration, high-risk country exposure, cross-border complexity)
3. pep_exposure — Are any principals, beneficial owners, or associated parties politically exposed persons (PEPs)?
4. source_of_funds — How clearly documented and credible is the stated source of investment funds? (vague descriptions, no supporting docs, red flags)
5. adverse_media_sanctions — Are there any sanctions hits, adverse media mentions, or pre-screening flags in the application or associated parties?
6. application_completeness — Is the application complete? (missing docs, unanswered questions, refusal to provide standard information)

For EACH dimension:
- findings: State exactly what the application data says (or fails to say) about this dimension. Be specific — quote or paraphrase actual fields.
- reasoning: Explain why those findings result in the stated risk_contribution level.
- risk_contribution: none | low | medium | high

Then synthesize an overall_risk_level and recommended_review_track.
</task>

- Base your analysis solely on the data provided. Do not invent information.

- estimated_aum should inform proportionality — larger AUM = higher inherent risk from a financial crime perspective.
- If tool results are available in the conversation (sanctions checks, PEP checks, jurisdiction lookups), incorporate them into the relevant dimensions.
- Every compliance_flag must be specific and actionable — e.g., "Nominee directors identified — UBO cannot be verified from submitted documents" rather than "ownership concern."
- missing_information should list concrete items the compliance team should request.
- Calibrate carefully: a standard US institutional client with full documentation should score low. Reserve critical for genuine red flags (active sanctions match, refusal to provide documents, explicit PEP with no enhanced DD).
